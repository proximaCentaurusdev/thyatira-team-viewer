About Thyatira Team

Thyatira Team is a theater group that writes, rehearses, and stages original productions, then brings them to a wider audience through this viewer. Every season is filmed and produced with the same care given to the performance itself.

This site exists so the people who support the team can watch from anywhere — a season pass and an access code stand in for a seat in the room.

— The Thyatira Team
